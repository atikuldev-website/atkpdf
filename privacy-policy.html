Privacy Policy – Atk PDF

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Atk PDF ("the app") is an image-to-PDF converter published by AtikulDev. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access. We do not collect names, email addresses, location, device identifiers or usage data.

Photos and files

  • You choose images with the Android system Photo Picker or take a photo with your camera app. The app only receives the specific images you select.
  • Images are processed entirely on your device to create PDF files.
  • Created PDFs are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • A PDF only leaves the app when you choose to Share it or Save to device. The app or location you choose then handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the PDFs stored inside it.

Permissions

The app does not request any runtime permissions. It does not use the internet, storage, media, camera, location, contacts or microphone permissions.

Third-party services

The app does not include any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
